Overview

Loopcast is a concept podcast player built around a simple idea: show notes and timestamped clips deserve to be first-class citizens, not an afterthought buried in an app description.

The problem

Most podcast apps treat episode notes as static text and make clipping a moment nearly impossible. I wanted to explore what a player designed around sharing specific moments could look like.

What I built

I started in Figma with a full flow — browse, listen, clip, share — then built the core player and clip-sharing interaction as a working React prototype to test the timestamp-linking feature.

  • Tap-to-clip moments with auto-generated shareable links
  • Interactive, timestamp-linked show notes
  • Custom waveform scrubber component
  • Full design system built in Figma before development

What I learned

Working through the full design-to-prototype pipeline myself sharpened how I think about handoff — designing components with their eventual code structure already in mind.
